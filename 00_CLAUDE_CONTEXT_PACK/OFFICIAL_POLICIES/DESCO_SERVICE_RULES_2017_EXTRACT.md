## 2. Crucial Sections for AIPMS Integration

### §3.3 Promotion Policies and Performance Appraisal Link
Promotion policy is designed to advance competent employees based on merit, efficiency, and seniority without bias. Promotion is contingent upon sanctioned vacancies. The Administrative Department maintains merit lists based on performance appraisal ratings and service records for approval by the competent authority.

### §4.8 Annual Increment / Pay Raise
Annual increment/pay raise is directly determined by the rating obtained in the Performance Appraisal (ACR). Employees receiving unsatisfactory performance evaluations or undergoing disciplinary punishment may have increments withheld per Service Rules.

### §7.1 Fidelity and Secrecy
Employees must maintain strict secrecy regarding company affairs and must not communicate company information obtained through their duties to unauthorized persons. A signed declaration of fidelity and secrecy is mandatory upon joining.

### §7.2 Conduct and Discipline
Employees must maintain exemplary conduct, follow lawful orders of superiors, observe office decorum, ensure punctuality, protect company assets, and avoid any acts of moral turpitude, conflict of interest, or gross negligence.

### §7.4 Penalties
Penalties are divided into:
1. **Minor Penalties:** Censure, withholding of promotion or increment for a specified period, recovery from pay of whole or part of any pecuniary loss caused to the Company.
2. **Major Penalties:** Reduction to a lower post or pay scale, compulsory retirement, removal from service, dismissal from service.

### §7.7 Inquiry and Due Process
Formal penalties require framing of charges, written explanation/show-cause notice, constitution of an Inquiry Committee, formal hearing, and inquiry report. Disciplinary matters cannot be treated as finalized performance evidence until due process is fully concluded and approved by the competent appointing authority.
