## 5. Conflict Resolution Protocol

If two sources disagree or express conflicting rules:

1. **State the Conflict Explicitly:** Cite the exact file names, sections, and page/line numbers of both conflicting sources.
2. **Apply Precedence:** Determine which source holds higher rank per Section 4.
3. **Assess Architectural Impact:** Identify how the conflict affects data schemas, workflows, AI advisory boundaries, or security constraints.
4. **Isolate Institutional Policy Questions:** If the higher-priority source is ambiguous or silent, classify the issue as an **"Unresolved DESCO Policy Decision"**.
5. **Never Invent Rules:** AI models must NEVER silently invent or synthesize a compromise rule without flagging it for DESCO confirmation.

## 6. Critical Architectural Guardrails for AI Models

Any model operating within this codebase must respect the following core system axioms:

1. **Official ACR Integrity:**
   - Grade 1–11 uses the official 25-metric, 1–4 scale.
   - Grade 12–16 uses the official 20-metric, 1–5 scale.
   - Do not reweight, merge, rescale, or delete official metrics.
2. **Domain Boundary Rule:**
   - The 10 AI parent domains are internal routing and classification constructs only. They are not appraisal outputs and never receive annual scores.
3. **AI Advisory Role:**
   - AI is strictly advisory. Official appraisal decisions, scores, overrides, and pen-picture approvals belong exclusively to authorized human evaluators (Initiating Officer, Countersigning Officer, Certifying Authority).
4. **Evidence Provenance & Lifecycle:**
   - Keep clear separation between system-verified metrics, factual employee self-inputs, supervisor observations, and authorized disciplinary records.
   - Pending allegations or unverified complaints must never enter the appraisal scoring pipeline.
   - A single adverse event must not automatically dictate an annual negative rating; honor the opportunity-to-correct lifecycle.
5. **Information Categorization Standard:**
   Always categorize statements into one of the following:
   - **Confirmed Source Fact:** Explicitly documented in official sources (Rank 1–3).
   - **Current Working Decision:** Validated in R&D handoff (Rank 4).
   - **Architecture Recommendation:** Technical proposal needing sign-off (Rank 5–6).
   - **DESCO Policy Clarification Required:** Institutional ambiguity needing client resolution.
